The 30-second version, in words

My first ADHD-EEG model reached 92.19%SET ASIDE. Then I checked it. The training epoch had been chosen on the test fold, worth ~15 points, and a linear model on 25 features beat my graph network. I traced the rest of the signal to things the model could be reading instead of a brain: a 50 Hz artifact that survived notch filtering, recording length alone at AUC 0.656, and exclusions that removed controls more often (3.5× (Fisher p = 0.011)). The rebuilt pipeline reaches AUC 0.9334 ± 0.0149 under subject-independent evaluation. This work is ongoing and makes no diagnostic claim.

03SIGNAL

08WHAT IT MEANS

The most valuable result here is the list of things that were wrong with the earlier number.

What this shows

  • The protocol, not the model, explained the headline.
  • On this benchmark the honest floor is not chance.
  • A notch filter did not remove an artifact that differed by group.
  • Exclusion lists can encode the label.
  • A simple model matched or beat the complex one.

What this does not show

  • Results on any other benchmark. This is one cohort of 121 children.
  • Transfer. A frozen model did not transfer to an independent second cohort, and the frequency band that carried signal differed. That is part of why the work is not finished.
  • A diagnostic tool, clinical use or clinical validation. It is none of these.
  • Peer review. The paper is in preparation.
  • That this is the end of the work. It is ongoing.
