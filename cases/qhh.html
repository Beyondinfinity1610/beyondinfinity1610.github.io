The 30-second version, in words

During a research internship at VIT Chennai I worked on an EEG representation that keeps both the amplitude and the phase of the Hilbert-transformed signal and passes them to a variational quantum circuit, built in PennyLane. It is exploratory representation research. It is not a decoder, and no quantum advantage is claimed.

02ASSUMPTION· did not hold

Keeping phase might make the representation richer.

A hypothesis explored, never a finding. Many real-valued feature pipelines drop phase; this was an attempt to see what the representation looks like when it is kept.

07WHAT IT MEANS

Knowing when there is nothing to claim is part of research.

What this shows

  • A defined way to carry amplitude and phase of the analytic EEG signal into a quantum-compatible representation, studied as a representation.
  • That I will say exploratory when that is what it is.

What this does not show

  • Any performance. No number from this project is shown anywhere on this site.
  • Quantum advantage, or any advantage over classical methods.
  • Better decoding of EEG, or any clinical use.
  • Validation beyond one small public dataset.
  • The manuscript's contents. It is under review, so this page stays at the level of the idea.
