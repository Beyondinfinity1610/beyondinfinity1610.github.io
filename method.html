04Principles, in words

Read them

A high number is where the investigation starts.

I treat suspiciously high performance as a hypothesis to test.

Case 02 · Failure →

Check what was chosen using the test data.

Leakage lives in the protocol as well as the data: epochs, thresholds, even which subjects were excluded.

Case 02 · Failure →

Before asking what the model learned, ask what else differs between the groups.

Confounds first: an acquisition artifact, recording length, who was excluded.

Case 02 · Investigation →

Filtering is a claim, not a fix.

Measure whether the artifact is still there.

Case 02 · Investigation →

Complexity has to earn its place.

Against a simple model, evaluated the same way.

Case 02 · Failure →

Every addition has to beat a random version of itself.

One change at a time, failures kept in the record.

Case 01 · Change →Case 02 · Investigation →

A metric is an assumption about how the model will be used.

Score on the stream the model would face, and print the alarm rate next to the sensitivity.

Case 01 · Investigation →

One split is an anecdote.

Repeat the partition, report the spread, and say what the ± means.

Case 02 · Result →

Compare under their protocol. If it is a tie, say it is a tie.

Where my convention and the comparator's differ, I use theirs.

Case 02 · Result →

In my projects the gains came from removing things.

A nuisance, a dead module, an inflated protocol, more often than from adding something.

Case 01 · Change →Case 02 · Change →

Set the number aside, and say so.

The earlier headline is on the record with its gap. So are two ideas that failed in the seizure rebuild.

Case 02 · Failure →

  • Question the metric. Control the idea. Validate on strangers.
  • Simple model first; complexity has to earn its place.
  • Report the failure next to the result.

Words I avoid

Claim discipline is mostly vocabulary. Each of these words says more than the work supports, so I use the right-hand version instead.

state of the art
Not claimed. I compare under the comparator's protocol and report the margin.
pre-registered
Pre-specified. The bar was written down in advance; it was not registered anywhere.
patented
Patent No. or Patent Application No., exactly as the number says.
published
Under review, or in preparation, as the case may be.
validated
Evaluated. Exploratory work stays labelled exploratory.
clinically useful
Not claimed. These are models evaluated on benchmarks, not tools.
real-world
Synthetic, when it is synthetic.
