The work in seven steps

  1. 01

    Case 01: The model that looked multimodal

    Seizure detection from a wearable. Under review at npj Digital Medicine.

  2. From outside, a wearable records four signals.

    Brain, heart, muscle and movement, from patients with epilepsy. The obvious plan is to fuse them and let the extra signals help.

  3. Three of them counted for nothing.

    I looked inside the model instead of tuning it. The fusion weights on heart, muscle and movement were 0.000. Exactly zero, not small.

  4. And it was barely looking.

    It was reading 2 s of each 17 s window. The first model looked reasonable. The evidence disagreed.

  5. So I rebuilt it, one stage at a time.

    Then I tested it on patients it never saw in training. It beat both baselines released with the dataset.

    Open this case

  6. 02The number that didn't survive its audit

    Then I audited my own best number.

    An ADHD classifier of mine had scored 92.19%SET ASIDE. The epoch behind it had been chosen on the test fold, so I set it aside.

    EEG and ADHD. Ongoing, paper in preparation.

  7. A stopwatch beat chance.

    Recording length alone, with no EEG, scored AUC 0.656. Rebuilt with subject-independent evaluation: AUC 0.9334 ± 0.0149, against 0.905 ± 0.013 for the strongest comparator I could verify. The margin is in AUC, not accuracy.

    Open this case

  8. 03The benchmark I built myself

    I wrote the exam first.

    Same discipline, different noise. I built a transaction simulator with scripted life events, then made the detector beat four controls before it counted: F1 above 0.75 on 3 of 4 rare events, on synthetic data.

    Synthetic transactions. Internship work.

    Open this case

  9. 04An exploration, labelled as one

    Can phase survive a quantum circuit?

    Back to EEG. It carries amplitude and phase; the Hilbert transform separates them, and a variational circuit tries to carry both. Exploratory and labelled so: no quantum advantage claimed, no performance claim.

    Under review at Frontiers in AI.

    Open this case

  10. Every result gets the same treatment.

    A question, a baseline, a control, an ablation, a confound check, a validation, and a report that keeps the failures in.

    How the method works

  11. Off the clock, the same brain.

    I sing, and I follow cars, cricket and House M.D., a show about not trusting the first explanation.
